Company

AI in service of people.

Arcadia ImmTech builds the AI case workspace and AI paralegal for U.S. immigration law firms. Headquartered in Los Angeles, California.

The dot

The dot is the person: the client, the attorney, the family waiting on a decision. The letter holds it up. Close, never merged.

That's how we build Arcadia. AI carries the work. The person stays on top.

Our name

Named for a place of peace and harmony.

We build Arcadia for the firms that guide people through the hardest part of making a life in America: immigration, made human and approachable.

  • Who we serveSmall U.S. immigration firms, from one to ten attorneys, and the paralegals who keep them running.
  • Our promiseDays, not weeks: a verified, complete filing package, ready for attorney review and signature.
