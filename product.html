Features

Your whole practice, in one place.

A glimpse of Arcadia, with fictional sample cases. The full tour is reserved for a private demo.

01 · Case command center

Every case at a glance.

What's ready for review, what's waiting on the client, and what's filed and being watched. Anyone on your team can pick up any case.

  • Cases grouped by where they stand
  • What needs a person comes first
  • Filed cases keep being watched

02 · Smart document intake

Drop in a pile of files from anywhere.

Arcadia names them, recognizes whose they are and files each to the right case for your team to confirm.

  • Photos, scans and PDFs from text, email and chat apps
  • Named and matched to the right client
  • Nothing is filed until someone confirms

03 · Ready for attorney review

Each case climbs to 100%.

Documents in, issues resolved, USCIS forms drafted from your client's information. Your attorney reviews a finished packet, not an unfinished file.

  • Every document checked against every other
  • Forms drafted from the case's own papers
  • Every answer shows where it came from

04 · Deadline watch

Every date in sight.

Arcadia reads the dates printed on every notice and document and puts them in front of your team before they matter, with your team reminded well ahead.

  • Appointments and response dates
  • Documents that are about to expire
  • Each date linked to the notice it came from

05 · Arcadia AI

An assistant that works ahead.

Arcadia AI answers questions about any case with the page it came from, and speaks up before anyone asks.

  • Ask about any caseType a question in plain words. The answer comes from the case's own papers.
  • Every answer shows its sourceThe document and page it came from, one click away.
  • It speaks up firstDates coming up, files a client just sent, a case ready for review: your team hears before anyone asks.
  • It knows where the line isLegal questions go to your attorney. The AI never answers them.

06 · Your clients, in their language

One conversation, in two languages.

One group chat per case and a simple client view in English, Spanish or Chinese, translated for your team.

  • Translated both ways for your team
  • Every message confirmed by your staff before it's sent
  • Clients see one task at a time
