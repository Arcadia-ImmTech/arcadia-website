The case workspace

Only what needs a person.

The case's own timeline and its source pages on one side; on the other, the few things that need a decision, each pinned to the page it came from.

  • The packet stays locked while two sources disagree
  • Mark OK records who decided, why and when
  • Details from the client's messages arrive as answers to confirm

USCIS forms

Filled on USCIS's own forms.

The I-130 and I-485 are drafted from the documents, the client's questionnaire and your call notes, then filled into USCIS's official PDF, current edition, with its protection left exactly as USCIS made it.

  • Every answer one click from its document and page
  • Only settled answers go in; open ones are listed for you
  • Signature boxes always left empty, for ink

Documents

Every file named and in its place.

Drop a pile of files from anywhere. Arcadia works out whose each one is and what it is, names it by what it is and whose it is, and files it to the right case for your team to confirm.

  • Phone photos, scans and email attachments, read the same way
  • One folder per case, with a trash you can restore from
  • Clients' uploads checked before anyone opens them

Messages

One chat per case, in the client's language.

Clients write in English, Spanish or Chinese; your team reads it in English, with the original a tap away. When a client mentions a date or a name, it arrives as a suggested answer for the forms.

  • Every message to a client confirmed by your staff first
  • Every AI translation marked as one
  • Clients join only when your firm invites them

Performance

See the hours Arcadia gives back.

Every document read, form answer drafted and call written up is counted against a paralegal's time for the same task, less the time your team spends reviewing it, at the hourly rate you set.

  • Day, week, month and year
  • Where the time came from, task by task
  • How every estimate is counted, one click away
