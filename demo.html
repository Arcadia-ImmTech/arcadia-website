Questions

Good to know

Does Arcadia give legal advice?

No. Arcadia is not a law firm. It organizes, checks, drafts and tracks for your firm, and brings every judgment call to your attorneys with the facts and their sources. Your attorneys make every legal decision.

Which cases can Arcadia handle?

Arcadia is built around USCIS's forms and the case work immigration firms do every day, and it keeps growing with them. In your demo we'll focus on the cases your firm handles most.

How is our clients' information handled?

Client information is used only to serve your firm's cases, under a written agreement with your firm. It's never sold and never used for advertising. Please don't send client documents or case details through this website or by email before we've agreed how to share them.

Do our clients have to learn new software?

Hardly. Clients see a simple view in English, Spanish or Chinese, with one task at a time and a group chat with your team. They only use it when your firm invites them.

How is Arcadia priced?

Flat and predictable, and never a share of your legal fees. We'll talk through what fits your firm in your demo.

How do we get started?

Request a private demo. We'll walk you through Arcadia with your kind of cases and talk about how it fits your team.
