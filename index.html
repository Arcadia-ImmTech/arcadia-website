The problem

Your team's best hours go to cross-checking paper.

Chasing documents

Checking by hand

A name spelled two ways, a date that doesn't match, a prior marriage with no divorce decree. Small details, easy to miss across dozens of pages.

Assembling the packet

Matching every document to the checklist and building the index takes time your attorneys could spend on judgment.

How it works

AI reads. Code checks. Your attorney decides.

  1. Your firm

    Share the case documents

    Passports, certificates, records and relationship evidence, as PDFs or phone photos, through your firm's own secure file sharing.

  2. Arcadia

    Read, organize, check

    AI reads each document into structured fields and notes the page each one came from. Plain, tested code then compares them across documents and against your checklist.

  3. Your attorney

    Review and decide

    You receive an issues report, an organized packet index and the extracted data. Every finding cites its source. All legal judgment stays with you.

Every finding cites file and page Neutral wording, never a verdict Translations marked as drafts for a certified translator

What Arcadia checks

The details that slow a case down.

Each check is written as plain, tested code, so the same documents always give the same result.

  • Names across documentsSpelling, middle names and name order. Possible transliteration differences are flagged for review.
  • Dates and places of birthMatched across every document that states them.
  • Marriage detailsDate and place of marriage, consistent from document to document.
  • Prior marriagesEach one has a document ending it, dated before the current marriage.
  • Passport datesExpired or expiring soon, noted for your attention.
  • Shared-life evidenceAddresses on documents that name both spouses, with their dates.
  • Checklist coverageChecklist items with no matching document yet.
  • Document qualityUnreadable files, possible missing pages, and non-English documents without a certified translation.

Our principles

Arcadia informs. Attorneys advise.

No legal advice, ever

Arcadia extracts, organizes and flags. It never says whether someone qualifies or what to file. Those calls belong to licensed attorneys.

Built for law firms

We work for firms, not directly with the public. Your clients stay your clients.

Client documents, handled with care

Documents are used only to prepare your firm's case. They are never sold, never used for advertising, and kept only as long as needed.

Flat fees, never a share

Firms pay a flat fee per case. Arcadia never takes a percentage of attorney fees and never sells placement.
