How Arcadia works

Everything between your strategy and your signature.

You make the legal calls. Arcadia does the rest of the case: the collecting, reading, checking, drafting, assembling and watching that used to fill a paralegal's week.

01

Open a case from your own note.

Write your strategy the way you would brief a paralegal. Arcadia turns it into the forms, the documents to collect, the client's to-dos and the dates, and drafts the first message to your client in their language.

No intake checklist to build by hand
02

Documents arrive named, checked and filed.

Clients send photos from their phone. Arcadia checks that each one is legible, complete, current and the document you asked for, names it, and files it to the right case. A blurry page goes back to the client in seconds, in words your firm set in advance, not weeks later.

No chasing, renaming or re-uploading
03

Every page read. Every fact cross-checked.

Names, dates and addresses are compared across every document by tested code. When two papers disagree, it comes to you as a single line with both pages one click away, before USCIS sees it.

The mismatch found on day one, not at the interview
04

USCIS forms, drafted and sourced.

The I-130 and I-485 are filled on USCIS's own PDF forms, current edition, from the documents, the questionnaire and your call notes. Every answer shows where it came from. Only settled answers go in; signatures stay yours, in ink.

Hundreds of boxes you no longer type
05

A package ready to sign and mail.

Cover letter, G-28, fees, forms in USCIS's order and tabbed evidence, assembled for you. Arcadia marks every place to sign and checks that each signed page came back before it goes in the envelope.

Days, not weeks, to a complete filing
06

Watched after filing.

The parcel is tracked until the receipt notice. Every notice is read, every date goes on Today with your firm's lead time, and when a case is due for its next step, Arcadia puts it in front of you.

No deadline living in someone's head
