Sound familiar?

Immigration practice still runs on paper, inboxes and memory.

Every firm we talk to describes the same week: great people, buried in work that never shows up on an invoice.

  1. 01

    Documents everywhere

    Clients send photos by text, WeChat, WhatsApp and email. Someone has to find them, name them and file them to the right case.

  2. 02

    The same facts, typed again and again

    Names, dates and addresses re-entered by hand on form after form, for every client.

  3. 03

    Small errors, big consequences

    A misspelled name or a date that doesn't match across documents, caught late, or not at all.

  4. 04

    Deadlines in someone's head

    Appointments, response dates and expiring documents tracked on sticky notes, calendars and spreadsheets.

  5. 05

    “What's the status of my case?”

    Calls and messages that pull your team away from the work, often in a language not everyone on staff speaks.

  6. 06

    Know-how that lives with one person

    Cases stall when someone is out, and attorneys review unfinished files late at night.

How Arcadia works
