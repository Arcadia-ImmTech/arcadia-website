Privacy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Visiting this website

The Arcadia website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Our fonts and images are served from this site, so visiting it doesn't send your information to other companies. Like any website, the service that hosts it may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure.

Requesting a demo

The demo request form asks for your name, work email, law firm, role and the number of attorneys at your firm, and, if you choose, your phone number and a short message. When you send it, your request is emailed to Arcadia's company email, which is provided by Google Workspace, and reaches our founders.

We use these details only to reply to you and to talk with you about Arcadia. We don't sell them or share them for marketing. You can ask us to delete your request at any time.

The form also carries two hidden checks against automated spam: an empty field that only automated programs fill in, and the time the page was opened. Neither identifies you.

Please don't include client names, case details or other sensitive personal information in the form or in an email. If your firm works with us, documents are shared through a secure method we agree on in writing.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your message only to reply and to talk with you about Arcadia. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.

Arcadia's services for law firms

When Arcadia works for a law firm, the firm's client information is handled under a written agreement with that firm. It is used only to serve the firm's cases, is never sold or used for advertising, and is kept only as long as needed.
