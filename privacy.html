Privacy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This website collects nothing

The Arcadia website has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Our fonts and images are served from this site, so visiting it doesn't send your information to other companies. Like any website, the service that hosts it may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your message only to reply and to talk with you about Arcadia. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.

Please don't send client documents, case details or other sensitive personal information by email. If your firm works with us, documents are shared through a secure method we agree on in writing.

Arcadia's services for law firms

When Arcadia prepares cases for a law firm, the firm's documents are handled under a written agreement with that firm. They are used only to prepare the firm's case, are never sold or used for advertising, and are kept only as long as needed.
