Privacy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This website collects nothing on its own

The Arcadia website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Our fonts and images are served from this site, so visiting it doesn't send your information to other companies. Like any website, the service that hosts it may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site running and secure.

Requesting a demo

The two questions before you book are only used to show you the next step. Your answers aren't stored or sent anywhere.

If you choose a time, the button opens Google Calendar's booking page in a new tab. Nothing loads from Google until you click it. Google then handles the booking, including the name, email address and time you enter, under Google's own privacy policy, and shares the booking with us so we can meet with you. We use those details only to hold the demo and follow up with you about Arcadia.

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your message only to reply and to talk with you about Arcadia. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.

Please don't send client documents, case details or other sensitive personal information through this website or by email. If your firm works with us, documents are shared through a secure method we agree on in writing.

Arcadia's services for law firms

When Arcadia works for a law firm, the firm's client information is handled under a written agreement with that firm. It is used only to serve the firm's cases, is never sold or used for advertising, and is kept only as long as needed.
