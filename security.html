Where Arcadia stops

The decision always stays with a person.

In the workspace, terracotta appears only where a decision is needed: items awaiting attorney review, overrides, and the next step for a client. Everything else stays calm.

How Arcadia writes

State the fact. Cite the page. Leave the decision to the attorney.

Every sentence Arcadia writes follows the same rules, from issue reports to client messages. Short sentences, form numbers in full, and no terms a client wouldn't use.

Where Arcadia writes The rule
Issue report Name order differs between the passport, page 2, and the marriage certificate, page 1. States the difference and both pages. Never blames, never predicts.
Dashboard 3 items need attorney review. Counts what needs a person. Never decides for the attorney.
Client message We received your marriage certificate. Next, please send proof of a shared address. Says what arrived and what's next, in the client's language, confirmed by staff. Never guesses the outcome.
